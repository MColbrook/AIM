## 410 — Quadratic-growth rigidity for the complex Monge–Ampère equation

**Target:** [Exact statement at the reviewed revision](https://github.com/MColbrook/AIM/blob/8729eab98bafe409b66c5e39253bf56569a62327/problems/410-complex-monge-ampere-quadratic-growth.md). Catalogue badge at the snapshot: 🔵 OPEN.

**Primary source:** [Jianchun Chu and Yaxiong Liu, Quadratic growth rigidity for the complex Monge–Ampère equation, arXiv:2610.26016v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26016v1) (19 October 2026).

**Location:** Theorem 1.1.

**Reported answer:** Affirmative: every such solution is a quadratic polynomial.

**Full-target correspondence:** Theorem 1.1 assumes $`u\in\mathrm{PSH}(\mathbb C^n)\cap C^\infty`$ with $`\det(u_{i\bar j})=1`$, which forces strict plurisubharmonicity, and the same two-sided bound $`C^{-1}(1+|z|^2)\le u\le C(1+|z|^2)`$. It concludes that $`u`$ is a quadratic polynomial; since the complex Hessian is nondegenerate, the degree is exactly two in the $`2n`$ real coordinates. Neither completeness of the metric nor real convexity is assumed, and $`n`$ is unrestricted.
