## 595 — The minimum vertex number of a triangulation of real projective 5-space

**Target:** [Exact statement at the reviewed revision](https://github.com/MColbrook/AIM/blob/8729eab98bafe409b66c5e39253bf56569a62327/problems/595-rp5-minimum-vertices.md). Catalogue badge at the snapshot: 🔵 OPEN.

**Primary source:** [Xinrui Chang, The minimum number of vertices of a triangulation of RP^5, arXiv:2610.25171v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25171v1) (18 October 2026).

**Location:** Theorem 1.1; Sections 3–5 and the section on computational verification.

**Reported answer:** Affirmative: $`\mu_5=24`$.

**Full-target correspondence:** Theorem 1.1 states that every finite simplicial complex whose realization is homeomorphic to $`\mathbb{RP}^5`$ has at least $`24`$ vertices, with no combinatorial-manifold, symmetry or polytopality assumption. The topological reductions use only local-homology properties of triangulated manifolds. Together with the $`24`$-vertex triangulation cited on this page, this gives $`\mu_5=24`$ in the catalogue's sense.

**Review:** On clopen.solutions the paper is recorded against the same problem ([OP-00846](https://clopen.solutions/OP-00846/)), with a full claim. Its automated review used two AI referees: Claude Fable 5.1 on the argument and Claude Opus 5.5 on whether the theorem matches the problem as stated. Held on the completeness of the finite classifications behind the $`23`$-vertex case (uniqueness on $`16`$ vertices, the $`244/115`$ classes on $`17`$ vertices, the covering certificates for the $`115`$ critical classes). The paper summarizes these runs, and its ancillary files contain the final complexes and certificates but not the exhaustive enumeration records. The curator judged the topological reductions sound and accepted the computed catalogues as the paper's computer-assisted part; they have not been re-run.

**Evidence limits:** This is AI review, not human peer review, and the argument has not been independently audited here; no Lean verification is claimed. Under the [contribution policy](../../CONTRIBUTING.md#status-and-evidence) the entry is therefore Solution claimed, not Solved.
