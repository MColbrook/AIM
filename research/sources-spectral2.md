# Sources for spectral and mathematical-physics problems 096–120

Reviewed 2026-09-08. The first source below supplies an explicit open assertion or precise conjecture; individual entries identify the roles of additional references. Books and surveys were used to locate questions, then primary papers and recent resolution searches were checked. Numerical evidence is evidence for a conjecture, not its resolution.

- [101. Absolute continuity for smooth periodic scalar elliptic operators](../problems/094-periodic-elliptic-absolute-continuity.md): P. Kuchment, [An overview of periodic elliptic operators](https://doi.org/10.1090/bull/1528), Bulletin of the AMS 53 (2016), Conjecture 6.13: explicit open assertion, including smooth variable principal coefficients.

- [102. Irreducibility of complex Fermi varieties](../problems/095-complex-fermi-irreducibility.md): P. Kuchment, [An overview of periodic elliptic operators](https://doi.org/10.1090/bull/1528), Bulletin of the AMS 53 (2016), Conjecture 5.35: precise exceptional-energy formulation.

- [103. Sunada’s no-gap conjecture for abelian covers](../problems/096-sunada-no-gap-cover.md): P. Kuchment, [An overview of periodic elliptic operators](https://doi.org/10.1090/bull/1528), Bulletin of the AMS 53 (2016), §6.1.2, Definition 6.2 and Conjecture 6.4: covering and no-gap assertion.

- [104. Simon’s multidimensional weighted square-integrability conjecture](../problems/097-simon-weighted-l2.md): B. Simon, [Tosio Kato’s work on non-relativistic quantum mechanics: part 2](https://doi.org/10.1007/s13373-018-0121-5), Bulletin of Mathematical Sciences (2018), Conjecture 20.2: weighted condition and infinite multiplicity.

- [105. A universal bound on excess molecular charge](../problems/098-maximal-molecular-ionization.md): M. Lewin, [Some open mathematical problems concerning charged quantum particles](https://doi.org/10.5802/crphys.249), Comptes Rendus Physique 26 (2025), Open Problem 1 and §2.1.2: exact target and known bounds.

- [106. Uniform boundedness of the first atomic ionization energy](../problems/099-uniform-first-ionization.md): J. P. Solovej, [A new look at Thomas–Fermi Theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/1601.00497), Molecular Physics 114 (2016), discussion of the ionization conjecture and equation defining $`I_m(Z)`$: explicit bounded-ionization-energy problem.

- [107. Convexity of atomic ground-state energy in electron number](../problems/100-atomic-energy-convexity.md): M. Lewin, [Some open mathematical problems concerning charged quantum particles](https://doi.org/10.5802/crphys.249), Comptes Rendus Physique 26 (2025), §2.1.3, equation (6), and discussion of Simon’s atomic Problem 10A: conjecture and limits of generalizations.

- [108. Concentration of nuclear charge minimizes the Dirac gap level](../problems/101-dirac-charge-concentration.md): M. J. Esteban, M. Lewin and É. Séré, [Dirac–Coulomb operators with general charge distribution II. The lowest eigenvalue](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.04051), Proceedings of the London Mathematical Society 123 (2021), §2.3, Conjecture 1, equation (30): exact charge-concentration conjecture.

- [109. Faber–Krahn inequality for graphene quantum dots](../problems/102-quantum-dot-dirac-faber-krahn.md): J. Duran, A. Mas and T. Sanz-Perela, [A connection between quantum dot Dirac operators and $`\overline\partial`$-Robin Laplacians in the context of shape optimization problems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.18698), preprint (2025; revised February 2026), Conjecture 1.1 and the paragraph following it: the massless infinite-mass-boundary problem.

- [110. The disk maximizes the first magnetic Neumann eigenvalue](../problems/103-magnetic-neumann-disk.md): B. Colbois, C. Léna, L. Provenzano and A. Savo, [A reverse Faber–Krahn inequality for the magnetic Laplacian](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.11336), Journal de Mathématiques Pures et Appliquées 192 (2024), Conjecture 1.5 and main theorem: Fournais–Helffer conjecture and moderate-field case.

- [111. Strict angle monotonicity of magnetic wedge ground energy](../cut_problems/problems/104-magnetic-wedge-angle-monotonicity.md): V. Bonnaillie-Noël, [Schrödinger operator with magnetic field in domain with corners](https://proceedings.centre-mersenne.org/articles/10.5802/jedp.15/), Journées Équations aux Dérivées Partielles (2005), §2, p. II–5: explicit strict-angle-monotonicity conjecture.

- [112. Baur’s high-field disk conjecture for all higher eigenvalues](../problems/105-baur-high-field-disk.md): M. Baur, [Numerical optimization of eigenvalues of the planar magnetic Dirichlet Laplacian with constant magnetic field](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0253311), Journal of Mathematical Physics 66 (2025), Conjecture 4.2: source of the threshold.

- [113. Is the optimal magnetic rectangle always a square?](../cut_problems/problems/106-optimal-magnetic-rectangle.md): D. Krejčiřík, [Is the optimal magnetic rectangle a square?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.16152), preprint (2025), main question: exact rectangle problem.

- [114. A sharp universal inequality for clamped-plate eigenvalues](../problems/107-clamped-plate-universal-bound.md): D. Chen, Q.-M. Cheng and G. Wei, [A gap for eigenvalues of a clamped plate problem](https://arxiv.org/abs/1610.05889), preprint (2016), Conjecture 1.1, equation (1.6): exact proposed inequality.

- [115. Uniqueness of the critical catenoid](../problems/108-critical-catenoid-uniqueness.md): R. Kusner and P. McGrath, [On Steklov Eigenspaces for Free Boundary Minimal Surfaces in the Unit Ball](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.06884), preprint (2020; revised 2022), Conjecture 1 and Theorem 1.1: conjecture and antipodal-symmetry case.

- [116. The first Steklov eigenvalue of a free-boundary minimal hypersurface](../problems/109-free-boundary-first-steklov.md): R. Kusner and P. McGrath, [On Steklov Eigenspaces for Free Boundary Minimal Surfaces in the Unit Ball](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.06884), preprint (2020; revised 2022), Conjecture 2: explicit hypersurface statement.

- [117. Linear growth of interior Steklov nodal volume](../problems/110-steklov-bulk-nodal-volume.md): B. Colbois, A. Girouard, C. Gordon and D. Sher, [Some recent developments on the Steklov eigenvalue problem](https://doi.org/10.1007/s13163-023-00480-3), Revista Matemática Complutense 37 (2024), Open Question 10.7: proposed interior and boundary nodal estimates; this entry takes the interior question.

- [118. Unbounded first Steklov multiplicity with fixed boundary count](../problems/111-steklov-fixed-boundary-multiplicity.md): S. Audet-Beaumont, [Constructing surfaces with first Steklov eigenvalue of arbitrarily large multiplicity](https://doi.org/10.4153/S0008439525101288), Canadian Mathematical Bulletin 69 (2026), Remark 1.3: explicit fixed-boundary-count question in the journal version.

- [119. Arbitrarily large Steklov frequency with a prescribed embedded boundary](../problems/112-fixed-embedded-boundary-steklov.md): B. Colbois, A. Girouard, C. Gordon and D. Sher, [Some recent developments on the Steklov eigenvalue problem](https://doi.org/10.1007/s13163-023-00480-3), Revista Matemática Complutense 37 (2024), §4.6, Open Question 4.52: prescribed-boundary submanifold problem.

- [120. Anderson localization for the cosine skew-shift model at every coupling](../problems/113-skew-shift-localization.md): D. Damanik, [Schrödinger operators with dynamically defined potentials](https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.2445), Ergodic Theory and Dynamical Systems (2017), §9.1, boxed “Skew-Shift-Problem”: explicit cosine-model question.

- [121. A universal central limit law for quantum-graph nodal surplus](../problems/114-quantum-graph-nodal-clt.md): L. Alon, R. Band and G. Berkolaiko, [Universality of nodal count distribution in large metric graphs](https://doi.org/10.1080/10586458.2022.2092565), Experimental Mathematics 33 (2024), Definitions 2.2–2.4 and Conjecture 3.1: distribution and full proposed law.

- [122. Jakobson–Naud’s essential spectral-gap conjecture](../problems/115-jakobson-naud-essential-gap.md): D. Jakobson and F. Naud, [On the resonances of convex co-compact subgroups of arithmetic groups](https://arxiv.org/abs/1011.6264), preprint (2010), §1, Conjecture 1.1: exact threshold; arithmetic assumptions belong to some partial results, not the conjecture.

- [123. Full-order resonance growth for every nonzero real compact potential](../problems/116-full-order-potential-resonances.md): M. Zworski, [Mathematical study of scattering resonances](https://doi.org/10.1007/s13373-017-0099-4), Bulletin of Mathematical Sciences 7 (2017), §2.7, Conjecture 1: full-order lower bound for real potentials.

- [124. Boundary contact of the second nodal set in convex domains](../problems/117-convex-higher-dimensional-payne.md): P. Freitas and R. Leylekian, [Payne’s nodal line conjecture fails on doubly-connected planar domains](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.24436), preprint (2025), introduction: explicitly identifies the higher-dimensional general convex problem as unresolved.

- [125. Subpolynomial compact-set peaks of Hecke–Maass waves](../problems/118-sarnak-compact-supnorm.md): Y. N. Petridis and M. S. Risager, [Averaging over Heegner points in the hyperbolic circle problem](https://doi.org/10.1093/imrn/rnx026), International Mathematics Research Notices (2018), §1, conjectural assumption (C2): compact-set sup-norm conjecture.
