## PR 171 and PR 175: AIM 458

Retain revised solution; no verified earlier complete match located.

- [Daniel Goodair, *Weak and Strong Solutions to Nonlinear SPDEs with Unbounded Noise*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.10076), 2024-01-18, Section 5.1, Theorems 5.1–5.2: The three-dimensional no-slip result is a weak martingale theorem. The stronger probabilistic result stated here is two-dimensional. This does not give the requested three-dimensional pathwise Besov local theorem.
- [Daniel Goodair, *Navier-Stokes Equations with Navier Boundary Conditions and Stochastic Lie Transport: Well-Posedness and Inviscid Limit*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.04290), 2023-08-08, Abstract and Navier boundary theorem: The strong theorem is two-dimensional with Navier boundary conditions; it does not establish the arbitrary smooth three-dimensional no-slip formulation.
- [Daniel Goodair, *Anisotropic Inviscid Limit for the Navier-Stokes Equations with Transport Noise Between Two Plates*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13199), 2026-03-13, Abstract; weak martingale approximation statement: The geometry is a two-plate channel and the approximating solutions are weak martingale solutions, with a different inviscid-limit conclusion.
- [Antonio Agresti and Mark Veraar, *Nonlinear SPDEs and Maximal Regularity: An Extended Survey*](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00030-025-01090-2), 2025, Open Problem 9; discussion before and after it: The source still asks the non-small-noise no-slip three-dimensional problem; its small-noise perturbation observation and periodic theory are narrower.

Fresh searches used the exact no-slip/transport-noise/local-well-posedness terms recorded in the JSON, and examined the actual no-slip theorem in Goodair Section 5.1. No earlier result located supplies the full three-dimensional pathwise local Besov theorem on every smooth bounded domain.
