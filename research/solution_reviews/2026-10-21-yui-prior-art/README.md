# Prior-art audit of yuiyuiui solution submissions

Reviewed 21 October 2026 by OpenAI Codex in separate literature-review contexts and a coordinating review. This audit checks priority and exact applicability, using earlier primary papers, preprints, publisher records and public proof sources. It is not a new whole-proof audit of every retained manuscript, human peer review, or formal verification.

## Scope and decisions

The complete authored-PR inventory contains **139 pull requests**: **123 original manuscript submissions**, three follow-up revisions or attribution repairs, one literature report covering 38 targets, and 12 closed/unmerged submissions. There are **166 distinct AIM targets** across those contributions. A fresh final GitHub inventory matched every pinned PR revision. The [submission inventory](submission-inventory.json) preserves the pre-audit metadata; the [decision ledger](decisions.json) records every disposition.

- **AIM 556 / PR 83:** replace the later contributor manuscript with [Esra Sengelen Sevim's existing solution](556-prior-solution.md), DOI 10.1016/j.jco.2026.102074. The PDF, TeX and obsolete package files are removed, and the entry remains **Solved**.
- **AIM 010 / PR 60:** the earlier correction to [Mikael Sundqvist's solution](../../solutions/010-mercedes-disk-partition/README.md), arXiv:2609.21964, is retained. The later Mercedes-star manuscript remains removed.
- **Other original manuscript targets:** no verified earlier complete matching solution was located in the searches recorded below. Their current statuses and hosted materials are retained. This is not a guarantee of novelty.
- **Previously attributed external work:** the 38-target report and closed literature submissions are identified separately. They do not become new original solutions by being included in a contributor PR. Existing claims and partial entries are not promoted to Solved.

Each original-manuscript search starts from the unchanged problem statement and the actual submitted claim, with earlier review comments used only as leads. A candidate replacement must predate the submission and cover the exact assumptions, quantifiers and conclusion. Different equations, noise classes, boundary conditions, partial parameter ranges, announcements without an available complete proof, and unsubstantiated proof-status pages are recorded with their limits.
