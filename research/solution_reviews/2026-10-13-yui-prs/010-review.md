# AIM 010 correction and existing solution

**Updated 2026-10-21:** The local manuscript from [PR 60](https://github.com/MColbrook/AIM/pull/60) has been removed and replaced by a [citation note](../../solutions/010-mercedes-disk-partition/README.md). The acceptance recommendation and AI review recorded here on 13 October 2026 are superseded.

The existing solution is Mikael Sundqvist, [The Y-partition is the optimal three-partition for the disc and the harmonic oscillator](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21964), arXiv:2609.21964, submitted 18 September 2026. Theorem 1.1 addresses the full disk target in [AIM 010](../../../problems/010-mercedes-disk-partition.md).

This correction records the earlier solution and the removal of the local package; it is not a new independent audit of Sundqvist's proof.
