# AIM 911: Sharp radial Dirac bounds and stability of the scalar spinor

**Author and submitter:** Matthew J. Colbrook, Department of Applied Mathematics and Theoretical Physics, University of Cambridge, United Kingdom. [mjc249@maths.cam.ac.uk](mailto:mjc249@maths.cam.ac.uk); [department profile](https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/person/mjc249).

**Status:** Partially resolved — Partial progress.

The exact constant is obtained on the specified two-channel radial class; the scalar Talenti spinor has nonnegative second variation in all compactly supported tangent directions.

**Remaining target:** Determine the global constant $`C_d`$ over all of $`\mathcal X_d`$ for every $`d\ge3`$. The radial theorem and local second-variation result do not prove a global inequality for arbitrary spinors.
