\begin{abstract}
We prove positive-time $L^\infty$ bounds for smooth rapidly decaying solutions of the spatially homogeneous Landau equation with Coulomb interaction in three dimensions. After fixing mass, momentum and energy, the bound on each positive waiting interval depends only on an upper bound for the initial entropy and on the time interval. It is independent of initial higher moments, Fisher information and pointwise bounds. A weighted Fisher estimate first gives concentration control compatible with the second moment. To control large velocities, we use two diffusion directions supplied by a bounded part of the distribution together with the full nonlinear collision energy. A tangential kernel identity yields a nonlinear Nash inequality, from which a local small-entropy argument excludes escaping density peaks. A local parabolic estimate then controls the bounded velocity region.
\end{abstract}
