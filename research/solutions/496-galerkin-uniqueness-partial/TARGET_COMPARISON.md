# AIM 496: target comparison

Target revision: `c4036871b0bf15cbc83e79c780b7191bcbb2cfed`. See [statement.md](statement.md).

The target quantifies over dimensions two and three, fixed measurable reactions with arbitrary scalar growth, and every conforming shape-regular quasi-uniform simplicial mesh family. It asks for uniqueness among all discrete solutions.

The planar potential-uniform stability theorem covers the complete two-dimensional target, including nonacute meshes. In three dimensions the manuscript proves that spurious branches must escape every bounded H1 set; it also proves uniqueness for f(x,t)=f(x,0)+c(x)g(t), with c in L2 strictly positive almost everywhere and g nondecreasing and locally Lipschitz. An energy domination condition is another sufficient case. These restrictions do not settle arbitrary three-dimensional reactions. The entry remains Partially resolved.
