# AI use and review scope

The manuscript was developed with substantial assistance from OpenAI Codex
in mathematical exploration, algebraic checking, proof exposition and writing.
Its author and contact are Kaiwen Jin and yui.ui.siki@gmail.com.

An earlier draft containing only the linear result received two separate AI
readings, one focused on exposition and one on mathematical referee questions.
Their applicable comments were addressed in the author revision. Those
readings did not cover the later combined linear-and-cubic manuscript.

The authoring AI checked the combined argument, cited source statements,
compilation, references and all 27 rendered pages. These checks are not an
independent human review or a formal proof. No Lean verification was performed,
and no separate detailed AI referee report is submitted.

Independent mathematical review of both proofs is requested. This submission
claims a partial result for Problem 541: a linear counterexample and cubic
convergence, with the quadratic case still unresolved. A successful build,
an AI check or a submitted pull request does not establish acceptance.
