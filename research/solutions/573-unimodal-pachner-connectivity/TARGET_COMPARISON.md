# Correspondence with AIM 573

Theorem 1 of *Unimodal Pachner paths for three-manifold triangulations* gives a proposed affirmative resolution of [AIM 573](https://github.com/MColbrook/AIM/blob/b2ec0fac559f532fc0ed04fce81deea0ce07c68b/problems/573-unimodal-pachner-connectivity.md).

| AIM 573 | Manuscript |
|---|---|
| Compact connected \(3\)-manifold with no \(2\)-sphere boundary | Theorem 1 has the same manifold hypotheses, with no orientation or irreducibility requirement. |
| Closed or ideal generalized triangulations | Theorem 1 uses finite affine facet pairings, with arbitrary vertex and edge coincidences and no reversed edge. The relative replacements in Section 2.1 account for exterior identifications and preserve the vertex links, including the links to be truncated. |
| Same number of internal vertices, possibly zero | In Theorem 1 the internal vertices are the vertices with sphere links. Their counts are allowed to be zero. |
| At least two tetrahedra in each triangulation | The same endpoint condition occurs in Theorem 1 and in the cited connectivity result. |
| A finite sequence of \(2\)--\(3\) moves followed by \(3\)--\(2\) moves, either part possibly empty | Theorem 1 yields two \(2\)--\(3\) paths to the same triangulation. Following one and reversing the other gives the stated sequence. |

The proof uses arbitrary-order Matveev–Piergallini–Amendola connectivity and copies a negative move together with its following positive tail. The added arch is shortened using one positive move while the base is advanced by two positive moves. The accompanying verifier reproduces the fixed finite face-pairing calculations. The proof of termination and the reduction to arbitrary input triangulations are in the manuscript.
