# Source map for continuum and materials problems 221–244

Checked on **2026-09-13**. Each entry combines a published open question or research question with a precise formulation and a separate search for later proofs, counterexamples and narrower results. The status is a literature-search finding, not a certification that a problem remains unsolved. Exact search topics and qualifications appear in each problem’s status review.

The source set includes books and chapters on elasticity (Ball), composites (Milton), microscopic heat transport (Bonetto–Lebowitz–Rey-Bellet), and applied knot theory (Denne), alongside individual research papers. No entry targets numerical linear algebra. Where a general published question is instantiated with fixed coefficients or a specified solution class, the entry identifies that formulation choice.

## Coverage and distinctions

Existing problems 001–195 and the other new batches were checked for overlap. The liquid-crystal entries concern distinct order descriptions and questions: orientational-distribution symmetry, director defects, and tensor realizability. The kinetic entries concern aggregation/fragmentation, inhomogeneous elastic-gas uniqueness, and the profile of a freely cooling inelastic gas. The wave entries separate nonlinear water-wave stability, scalar-field kink relaxation, and unbounded growth along one periodic Schrödinger trajectory.

The exclusion record is [excluded-continuum3.md](excluded-continuum3.md). Several September 2026 preprints were checked because a search restricted to older surveys would have retained questions that now have a reported resolution. Their preprint status is retained in the record.
