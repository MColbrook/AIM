# 844. Do monotone tori minimize Hamiltonian volume in complex hyperbolic space?

**Area:** Geometry and topology
**Status:** 🟡 PARTIAL
**Last checked:** 2026-10-30

## Problem statement

Equip complex hyperbolic space $`\mathbb{C}H^n`$ with its standard Kähler form $`\omega`$ and metric $`g`$ of holomorphic sectional curvature $`-4`$. In the unit-ball model, define
```math
\Phi(z)=\frac{z}{\sqrt{1-|z|^2}},\qquad
T_a=S^1(a)\times\cdots\times S^1(a)\subset\mathbb{C}^n,\qquad
L_a=\Phi^{-1}(T_a),\quad a>0.
```

Thus $`L_a`$ is the embedded Lagrangian torus whose coordinate moduli are all $`a/\sqrt{1+na^2}`$.

For every integer $`n\ge2`$ and every $`a>0`$, is
```math
\mathrm{Vol}_g\bigl(\Psi(L_a)\bigr)
\ge \mathrm{Vol}_g(L_a)
=(2\pi)^n a^n\sqrt{1+na^2}
```

true for every $`\Psi\in\mathrm{Ham}_c(\mathbb{C}H^n,\omega)`$?

Here $`\mathrm{Ham}_c`$ consists of time-one maps generated by smooth time-dependent Hamiltonians compactly supported in $`[0,1]\times\mathbb{C}H^n`$. Competitors need not preserve the torus action or remain near $`L_a`$.

## Application

This is a geometric variational problem: it asks whether second-variation stability extends to a global volume minimum within a Hamiltonian isotopy class. No direct practical application is identified here.
