# 843. Unknotting number and genus of positive fibered knots

**Area:** Geometric topology and knot theory
**Status:** 🟡 PARTIAL
**Last checked:** 2026-10-30

## Problem statement

Let $`K\subset S^3`$ be a smooth knot. Suppose that $`K`$ admits an oriented diagram with every crossing positive and that its exterior fibers over $`S^1`$, with a Seifert surface as fiber. Must
```math
u(K)=g(K)?
```


Here $`g(K)`$ is the minimum genus of a connected compact orientable surface embedded in $`S^3`$ with boundary $`K`$. The unknotting number $`u(K)`$ is minimized over **all diagrams** and crossing-change sequences, allowing isotopy between changes. Positivity is a property of the knot, rather than a prescribed diagram.

## Application

This asks whether a geometric surface invariant determines the minimum crossing-change complexity throughout the class of positive fibered knots. It extends a known equality for closures of positive braids.

## Known progress

The lower bound $`u(K)\ge g(K)`$ is known in this class. Kegel and collaborators obstruct three approaches to proving the opposite bound. Their Theorem 1.5 concerns the crossings of a particular genus-seven knot diagram: that diagram needs nine changes. The unrestricted value nine is separately proposed in Conjecture 1.6, rather than proved. Thus their diagram theorem does not settle the question above.
