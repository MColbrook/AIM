# 931. Do isomorphic duals determine homogeneous polynomial spaces?

**Area:** Infinite-dimensional complex analysis and Banach spaces
**Status:** 🟡 PARTIAL
**Last checked:** 2026-10-30

## Problem statement

Let $`X`$ and $`Y`$ be complex Banach spaces whose continuous duals $`X^*`$ and $`Y^*`$ are isomorphic as Banach spaces. For an integer $`m\ge2`$, let $`\mathcal P({}^mX)`$ be the Banach space of continuous scalar-valued $`m`$-homogeneous polynomials on $`X`$, with norm $`\|P\|=\sup_{\|x\|\le1}|P(x)|`$.

Must $`\mathcal P({}^mX)`$ and $`\mathcal P({}^mY)`$ be isomorphic for every $`m\ge2`$? An isomorphism means a bounded complex-linear bijection with bounded inverse; it need not arise from a prescribed extension operator.

## Application

The dual describes continuous linear measurements on a space. This question asks how much those measurements determine about spaces of homogeneous nonlinear observables. It is a foundational question about duality and analytic function spaces.
