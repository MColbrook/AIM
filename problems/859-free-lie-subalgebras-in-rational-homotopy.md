# 859. Free Lie subalgebras in rational homotopy

**Area:** Rational homotopy theory
**Status:** 🟡 PARTIAL
**Last checked:** 2026-10-30

## Problem statement

Let $`X`$ be a simply connected finite CW complex. Give
```math
L_X=\bigoplus_{n\geq1}\pi_n(\Omega X)\otimes_{\mathbb Z}\mathbb Q
```
the graded Lie bracket induced by the Samelson product. Assume that $`L_X`$ has infinite total dimension over $`\mathbb Q`$; equivalently, $`X`$ is rationally hyperbolic.

Must $`L_X`$ contain a free graded Lie subalgebra on two generators? Explicitly, must there exist homogeneous $`u,v\in L_X`$ such that the homomorphism from the free graded Lie algebra over $`\mathbb Q`$ on generators of degrees $`|u|`$ and $`|v|`$, sending those generators to $`u,v`$, is injective?

No prescribed parity or equality of the generator degrees is required. This is the classical simply connected finite-complex Avramov-Félix conjecture. Broader versions for finite rational category or nonsimply connected spaces are not part of this entry.

## Application

The conjecture asks for a free algebraic structure inside the rational homotopy of every hyperbolic finite complex. It would clarify the structure underlying the already known exponential growth of rational homotopy groups.
