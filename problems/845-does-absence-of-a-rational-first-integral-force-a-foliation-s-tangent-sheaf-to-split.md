# 845. Does absence of a rational first integral force a foliation's tangent sheaf to split?

**Area:** Holomorphic foliations and algebraic geometry
**Status:** 🟡 PARTIAL
**Last checked:** 2026-10-30

## Problem statement

Let $`\mathcal F`$ be a codimension-one holomorphic foliation on complex projective three-space $`\mathbb P^3`$. Suppose its rank-two tangent sheaf $`T_{\mathcal F}`$ is locally free. If $`\mathcal F`$ has no invariant hypersurface, must $`T_{\mathcal F}`$ split as a direct sum of line bundles? The source also asks the stronger question with the weaker assumption that $`\mathcal F`$ has no rational first integral.

Both clauses are Problem 3 of da Costa's published article. They concern holomorphic *foliations*, not arbitrary nonintegrable distributions, and the ambient space is $`\mathbb P^3`$.

## Application

Splitting makes the tangent sheaf's geometry explicit and helps organize families of foliations on projective space. The added hypotheses test whether known nonsplit examples depend on their rational first integrals.
