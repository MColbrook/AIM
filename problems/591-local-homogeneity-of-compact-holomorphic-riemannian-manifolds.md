# 591. Local homogeneity of compact holomorphic Riemannian manifolds

**Area:** Complex differential geometry
**Status:** 🟡 PARTIAL
**Last checked:** 2026-10-30

## Problem statement

Let $`X`$ be a compact connected complex manifold and
```math
g\in H^0\!\left(X,\mathrm{Sym}^2T^*X\right)
```

an everywhere nondegenerate holomorphic symmetric complex-bilinear form.

Must $`g`$ be locally homogeneous? Precisely, for every $`x,y\in X`$, must there be neighborhoods $`U\ni x`$, $`V\ni y`$ and a biholomorphism $`f:U\to V`$ such that $`f(x)=y`$ and $`f^*(g|_V)=g|_U`$?

This is Biswas–Dumitrescu Conjecture 1.1. Equivalently, local holomorphic Killing fields span $`T_xX`$ at every point. Neither Hermitian positivity nor global transitivity is imposed.

## Application

The question asks whether a nondegenerate holomorphic metric on a compact complex space forces the same local metric geometry at every point.
