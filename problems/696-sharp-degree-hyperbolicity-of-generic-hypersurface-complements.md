# 696. Sharp-degree hyperbolicity of generic hypersurface complements

**Area:** Complex analysis and potential theory
**Status:** 🟡 PARTIAL
**Last checked:** 2026-10-30

## Problem statement

For integers $`n\ge2`$ and $`d\ge2n+1`$, consider the parameter space
```math
\mathcal P_{n,d}=\mathbb P\!\left(H^0(\mathbb P^n_{\mathbb C},\mathcal O(d))\right).
```

Does there exist a nonempty Zariski-open subset $`U_{n,d}\subset\mathcal P_{n,d}`$ such that every hypersurface $`H`$ parametrized by $`U_{n,d}`$ is smooth and $`\mathbb P^n_{\mathbb C}\setminus H`$ is Kobayashi hyperbolic?

The conclusion means that the Kobayashi pseudodistance on the complement separates distinct points. The boundary is a single smooth hypersurface. Generic means a Zariski-open condition, rather than only a very general condition. Hyperbolic embedding is not an additional conclusion requested here.

## Application

This is the logarithmic counterpart of hypersurface hyperbolicity: it asks how the degree of an omitted algebraic boundary constrains holomorphic maps and the intrinsic complex geometry of its complement.
