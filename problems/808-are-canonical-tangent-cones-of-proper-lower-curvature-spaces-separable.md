# 808. Are canonical tangent cones of proper lower-curvature spaces separable?

**Area:** Metric geometry and synthetic curvature
**Status:** 🟡 PARTIAL
**Last checked:** 2026-10-30

## Problem statement

Let $`\kappa\in\mathbb R`$ and let $`(X,d)`$ be a nonempty proper geodesic metric space with Alexandrov curvature bounded below by $`\kappa`$. Proper means that every closed bounded subset is compact; geodesic means that every pair of points can be joined by a minimizing geodesic. No finite-dimensionality assumption is imposed. Properness also makes $`X`$ complete and separable, as required by the source setting.

Here the curvature condition is lower triangle comparison: for every triangle of perimeter less than $`2D_\kappa`$, the distance from a vertex to a point on a chosen opposite geodesic side is at least the corresponding distance in the comparison triangle in the simply connected surface of constant curvature $`\kappa`$. Here $`D_\kappa=\infty`$ for $`\kappa\le0`$ and $`D_\kappa=\pi/\sqrt{\kappa}`$ for $`\kappa>0`$ [1, Definition 2.1].

For $`p\in X`$, consider nonconstant unit-speed minimizing geodesic germs from $`p`$. For two such germs $`\gamma,\eta`$, their Alexandrov angle is
```math
\alpha(\gamma,\eta)=\lim_{s,t\downarrow0}
\arccos\left(\frac{s^2+t^2-d(\gamma(s),\eta(t))^2}{2st}\right).
```

The lower curvature bound ensures the limit exists. Identify germs whose angle is zero and complete the resulting angular metric space, obtaining the space of directions $`\Sigma_pX`$. The **canonical tangent cone** is the Euclidean cone $`T_pX=C(\Sigma_pX)`$, with metric
```math
d_T(r\xi,s\zeta)^2=r^2+s^2-2rs\cos\alpha(\xi,\zeta),
\qquad r,s\ge0,
```

where all points with radius zero are identified. If there are no nonconstant germs, use the one-point cone.

Must $`T_pX`$ be **separable for every $`p\in X`$**? Equivalently, does each of these cone metric spaces have a countable dense subset?

This is the proper-space tangent-separability question discussed explicitly in [1, Remark 2.3], where its earlier formulation is attributed to Ohta. The canonical completion above is part of the question; an arbitrary pointed Gromov--Hausdorff blow-up or ultratangent is a different object in this generality.

## Application

Logarithmic maps send a point to the initial direction and length of a geodesic from a chosen base point. They provide tangent-valued objects used in the study of metric-space barycenters. Remark 2.3 of [1] constructs a log selection measurable for the sigma-field generated by closed balls in the tangent cone. Separability would make this the full Borel sigma-field, giving a Borel log selection.

This is a basic measurability question. It does not assert that every arbitrary choice of geodesic is measurable. The arguments in [1] already work with certain scalar functionals of the log map, and the flatness theorem in [2] avoids assuming the entire tangent cone is separable.

## Known results and scope

For a finite-dimensional smooth Riemannian manifold the tangent is a finite-dimensional Euclidean space, so separability holds. The question concerns the full class above, including potentially infinite-dimensional singular spaces.

The flatness result in [2] concerns the support of a pushed-forward probability measure at a barycenter. It does not establish separability of every entire tangent cone. Properness of $`X`$ must not be replaced by an unproved assertion that $`T_pX`$ is proper or that $`\Sigma_pX`$ is compact.

The conclusion here is countable density. The separate question whether every such canonical tangent is a length space has the same underlying geometric objects but a different conclusion.
