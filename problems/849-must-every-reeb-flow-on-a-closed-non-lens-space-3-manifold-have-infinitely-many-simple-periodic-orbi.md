# 849. Must every Reeb flow on a closed non-lens-space 3-manifold have infinitely many simple periodic orbits?

**Area:** Contact dynamics and low-dimensional topology
**Status:** 🟡 PARTIAL
**Last checked:** 2026-10-30

## Problem statement

Let $`\lambda`$ be any contact form on a closed three-manifold $`Y`$ that is not a lens space. Must its Reeb flow have **infinitely many geometrically distinct simple periodic orbits**?

The word *any* matters: the form may be degenerate, and the first Chern class of its contact structure need not be torsion.

## Application

This would rule out a Reeb flow with a finite number greater than two of simple periodic orbits on any non-lens-space three-manifold. It extends existing two-or-infinity results beyond their separate nondegeneracy and torsion-Chern-class settings.

## Status review


**Known cases:** Exactly-two-orbit classification does not rule out finite counts larger than two. The available two-or-infinity theorems require torsion first Chern class or a nondegeneracy hypothesis; the August 2026 result still has strong nondegeneracy. The problem removes both restrictions simultaneously.
