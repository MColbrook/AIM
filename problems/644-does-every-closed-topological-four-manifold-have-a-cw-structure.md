# 644. Does every closed topological four-manifold have a CW structure?

**Area:** Geometry and topology
**Status:** 🔵 OPEN
**Last checked:** 2026-10-30

## Problem statement

Is every closed topological four-dimensional manifold homeomorphic to a CW complex? Equivalently, can one give the manifold itself a CW cell structure, with its original topology?

Every compact manifold is *homotopy equivalent* to a finite CW complex. That weaker statement does not provide a CW structure on the manifold. Smooth and PL manifolds have CW structures, as do all compact manifolds of dimension at most three and all closed manifolds of dimension at least five. The closed topological four-dimensional case remains the question.

## Application

A CW structure would let cellular methods work directly on every closed topological four-manifold, including those without a smooth or PL structure. Distinguishing the manifold's own cell structure from its homotopy type matters in four-dimensional topology.
