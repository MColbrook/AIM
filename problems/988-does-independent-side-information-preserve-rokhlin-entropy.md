# 988. Does independent side information preserve Rokhlin entropy?

**Area:** Ergodic theory and dynamical information
**Status:** 🟡 PARTIAL
**Last checked:** 2026-10-30

## Problem statement

Let $`G`$ be countably infinite, with free probability-preserving actions on standard probability spaces $`(X,\mu)`$ and $`(Y,\nu)`$. Give $`X\times Y`$ the diagonal action and product measure.

For an invariant sigma-algebra $`\mathcal F`$ on $`(Z,\eta)`$, define

```math
h_G(Z,\eta\mid\mathcal F)=\inf_{\alpha}\mathrm H_\eta(\alpha\mid\mathcal F\vee\mathscr I_G),
\qquad
\sigma_G(\alpha)\vee\mathcal F\vee\mathscr I_G=\mathcal B(Z).
```


The infimum runs over countable measurable partitions satisfying the generation condition modulo null sets. Here $`\mathscr I_G`$ is the invariant sigma-algebra, $`\sigma_G(\alpha)`$ is generated by all translates of $`\alpha`$, and $`\mathrm H`$ is conditional Shannon entropy. Trivial $`\mathcal F`$ gives $`h_G(Z,\eta)`$.

Must

```math
h_G(X\times Y,\mu\times\nu\mid\pi_Y^{-1}\mathcal B(Y))=h_G(X,\mu)
```


always hold? Equality is in $`[0,+\infty]`$; no ergodicity or finite-entropy assumption is imposed.

The identity is known for amenable groups and when the second action is weakly contained in every free action, including Bernoulli shifts [1].

## Application

This tests whether an independent dynamical system can reduce the information needed to generate another system when its entire second coordinate is available.
