# 825. Does every tight contact 3-manifold have finite Giroux torsion?

**Area:** Contact and symplectic topology
**Status:** 🟡 PARTIAL
**Last checked:** 2026-10-30

## Problem statement

The *Giroux torsion* of a contact 3-manifold measures how many full twists of the contact planes can be embedded in a thickened torus $`T^2\times[0,1]`$. It is infinite if such embedded torsion domains exist with arbitrarily large twist number in the **same** contact manifold.

**Question.** Must every closed tight contact 3-manifold have **finite** Giroux torsion?

## Application

A positive answer would distinguish tight from overtwisted contact structures by finite versus infinite Giroux torsion. It would also clarify how much torsion tightness alone permits, beyond the zero-torsion results for strongly symplectically fillable contact structures.
