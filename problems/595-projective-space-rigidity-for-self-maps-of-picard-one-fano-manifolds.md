# 595. Projective-space rigidity for self-maps of Picard-one Fano manifolds

**Area:** Complex algebraic geometry and complex dynamics
**Status:** 🟡 PARTIAL
**Last checked:** 2026-10-30

## Problem statement

Let $`X`$ be a smooth connected complex projective variety of dimension $`n\ge1`$, with ample anticanonical bundle $`-K_X`$. Assume its Picard number is one:
```math
\rho(X)=\dim_{\mathbb R}N^1(X)_{\mathbb R}=1,
```

where $`N^1(X)_{\mathbb R}`$ is the space of real Cartier divisor classes modulo numerical equivalence.

If a surjective morphism $`f:X\to X`$ has degree greater than one, must
```math
X\cong\mathbb P^n_{\mathbb C}\,?
```

The degree is the generic number of preimages counted with multiplicity. The question ranges over all dimensions and all such morphisms.

## Application

The conjecture asks whether noninvertible algebraic dynamics characterizes projective space within this class of positively curved algebraic varieties.

## Known progress

Reference [1] records the surface and threefold cases and several geometric subclasses. Reference [2], Section 1.2, still states the general conjecture and discusses tangent-bundle positivity as a possible route.

Two 2026 results have narrower scope: [3], Theorem 1.5, assumes that the map is **Galois**; [4], Corollary 1.4, treats the currently known families of smooth Fano fourfolds of Picard rank one. Neither statement supplies the unrestricted conclusion above.
