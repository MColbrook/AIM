# 1001. Uniqueness of the invariant law for a stochastic one-dimensional compressible fluid

**Area:** Stochastic fluid dynamics
**Status:** 🟡 PARTIAL
**Last checked:** 2026-10-30

## Problem statement

On $`I=(0,1)`$, consider the Itô system
```math
\begin{aligned}
d\rho+(\rho u)_x\,dt&=0,\\
d(\rho u)+(\rho u^2+p(\rho))_x\,dt
&=u_{xx}\,dt+\rho\sum_{k\ge1}\sigma_k(x)\,dW_t^k,
\end{aligned}
```

with $`u(t,0)=u(t,1)=0`$ and total mass $`\int_0^1\rho\,dx=1`$.
The $`W^k`$ are independent standard real Brownian motions. Fix any deterministic noise coefficients satisfying
```math
\sigma_k\in H^2(I)\cap H^1_0(I),\qquad
\sum_{k\ge1}\|\partial_{xx}\sigma_k\|_{L^2(I)}^2<\infty.
```

Use the strong-solution phase space
```math
\mathcal X=\left\{(\rho,u)\in H^1(I)\times H^1_0(I):
\rho(x)>0\text{ for every }x\in[0,1],\quad\int_0^1\rho\,dx=1\right\},
```

with its $`L^2(I)\times L^2(I)`$ metric and Borel sigma-algebra. Positivity refers to the continuous representative of $`\rho`$; no single lower bound is imposed on every state in $`\mathcal X`$.

For every fixed $`A>0`$, set $`p(\rho)=A^2\rho`$. Does the Markov semigroup $`P_t`$ generated by the strong solutions have exactly one invariant Borel probability measure on $`\mathcal X`$? Invariance means
```math
\int_{\mathcal X}P_t\varphi\,d\mu=\int_{\mathcal X}\varphi\,d\mu
\quad(t\ge0)
```

for every bounded Borel test function $`\varphi`$. Existence is known. No rate of convergence to equilibrium or nondegeneracy of the noise is added to the question.

## Application

A unique stationary law would make the long-time statistical description of this noisy compressible fluid independent of the initial distribution.

## Status review


**Known cases:** The assertion is uniqueness of the invariant law for the full nonlinear interval flow, with any permitted noise. Pathwise solution uniqueness and uniqueness of a linear acoustic limiting invariant law do not imply it.
