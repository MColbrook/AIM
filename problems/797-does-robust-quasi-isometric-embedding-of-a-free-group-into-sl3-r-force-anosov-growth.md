# 797. Does robust quasi-isometric embedding of a free group into SL3(R) force Anosov growth?

**Area:** Geometric structures, Lie groups and hyperbolic dynamics
**Status:** 🟡 PARTIAL
**Last checked:** 2026-10-30

## Problem statement

Let $`F_2=\langle a,b\rangle`$ be the free group on two generators. Write $`|w|`$ for the length of the reduced word $`w`$ in $`a,a^{-1},b,b^{-1}`$. For $`A,B\in\mathrm{SL}_3(\mathbb R)`$, let $`w(A,B)`$ be the matrix obtained by substitution. For a real invertible matrix $`g`$, let $`\sigma_1(g)\geq\sigma_2(g)\geq\sigma_3(g)>0`$ be its singular values for the standard Euclidean inner product.

Fix $`A_0,B_0\in\mathrm{SL}_3(\mathbb R)`$. Suppose that there is an open neighborhood $`U`$ of $`(A_0,B_0)`$ in $`\mathrm{SL}_3(\mathbb R)^2`$ such that for every $`(A,B)\in U`$ there are constants $`c_{A,B}>0`$ and $`C_{A,B}\geq0`$ satisfying
```math
\log\frac{\sigma_1(w(A,B))}{\sigma_3(w(A,B))}
\geq c_{A,B}|w|-C_{A,B}
\qquad\text{for every }w\in F_2.
```

Must there exist $`c>0`$ and $`C\geq0`$ such that
```math
\log\frac{\sigma_1(w(A_0,B_0))}{\sigma_2(w(A_0,B_0))}
\geq c|w|-C
\qquad\text{for every }w\in F_2?
```


Prove the implication or construct a counterexample. The hypothesis is robust quasi-isometric embedding; its constants may depend on the nearby pair. The conclusion is the singular-value characterization of a projective Anosov representation. Applying it to inverse words also gives the same lower bound for $`\log(\sigma_2/\sigma_3)`$.

These conventions agree with the geometric definitions. For determinant-one matrices, $`\log\sigma_1\leq\log(\sigma_1/\sigma_3)\leq3\log\sigma_1`$, and a fixed finite set of generators gives the upper word-length bound automatically. Thus the hypothesis is equivalent to quasi-isometric orbit growth throughout an open set of representations. Openness is taken in the full space of matrix pairs, with no invariant subspace or relation imposed.

## Application

This asks whether stable coarse geometric embedding in a real higher-rank Lie group forces a uniform hyperbolic structure. It is a higher-rank version of the stability questions motivated by Sullivan's theorem for Kleinian groups, and would clarify the boundary of the Anosov representation space.

## Known results and scope

Carvajales–Lessa–Potrie retain the free-group question as Problem 1.1. Their Theorem 1.5 constructs quasi-isometric non-Anosov representations of free groups of rank greater than two, but those examples are explicitly not robust. The associated two-generator example is also non-Anosov, with robustness left undecided. Theorem 1.7 gives a positive result for derived-from-Barbot reducible suspensions with the stated orientation hypothesis; their rank-one product theorem is another known case.

Tsouvalas's published counterexamples settle the broader question for general hyperbolic source groups negatively. They do not have source $`F_2`$ and target $`\mathrm{SL}_3(\mathbb R)`$. His 2026 preprint on virtually free groups still explicitly lists the real free-group question as open on p. 2. Its Theorem 1.1 concerns nonarchimedean fields. Theorem 1.2 uses virtually free groups with torsion: openness for their constrained representations does not establish openness in the full representation space of a finite-index free subgroup. Theorem 1.3 concerns the semigroup generated by $`A,A^{-1},B`$, with no $`B^{-1}`$, rather than all reduced words in $`F_2`$.
