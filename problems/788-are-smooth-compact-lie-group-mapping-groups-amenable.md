# 788. Are smooth compact-Lie-group mapping groups amenable?

**Area:** Geometry and topology
**Status:** 🟡 PARTIAL
**Last checked:** 2026-10-30

## Problem statement

Let $`X`$ be a compact smooth manifold and $`G`$ a compact simple Lie group. Give the pointwise-multiplication group $`C^\infty(X,G)`$ its natural $`C^\infty`$ topology. Is this topological group **amenable**? Equivalently, does every continuous action of $`C^\infty(X,G)`$ on a compact space admit an invariant Borel probability measure?

The question concerns the smooth topology. Amenability for a weaker topology on the same maps does not answer it.

## Application

The question arose from gauge theory: amenability of gauge transformation groups can help construct gauge-invariant states. A general answer would identify how the topology imposed on a smooth symmetry group affects its invariant-measure theory.
