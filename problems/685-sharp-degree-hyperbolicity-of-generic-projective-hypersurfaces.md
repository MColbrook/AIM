# 685. Sharp-degree hyperbolicity of generic projective hypersurfaces

**Area:** Complex analysis and potential theory
**Status:** 🟡 PARTIAL
**Last checked:** 2026-10-30

## Problem statement

For integers $`n\ge3`$ and $`d\ge2n-1`$, let
```math
\mathcal P_{n,d}=\mathbb P\!\left(H^0(\mathbb P^n_{\mathbb C},\mathcal O(d))\right)
```

be the parameter space of degree-$`d`$ complex projective hypersurfaces.

Is there a nonempty Zariski-open subset $`U_{n,d}\subset\mathcal P_{n,d}`$ such that every hypersurface $`X`$ parametrized by $`U_{n,d}`$ is smooth and Kobayashi hyperbolic?

Kobayashi hyperbolicity means that the Kobayashi pseudodistance separates distinct points. Since $`X`$ is compact, this is equivalent to every holomorphic map $`\mathbb C\to X`$ being constant. Here **generic** means a Zariski-open condition; **very general** allows removing countably many proper algebraic subsets.

## Application

This asks whether a linear degree threshold forces generic complex projective hypersurfaces to exclude entire holomorphic curves. It connects algebraic degree bounds with invariant complex metrics.
