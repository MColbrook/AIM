# 711. Unions of conformally removable compact sets

**Area:** Complex analysis, conformal geometry and potential theory
**Status:** 🟡 PARTIAL
**Last checked:** 2026-10-30

## Problem statement

A compact set $`E\subset\widehat{\mathbb C}`$ is *conformally removable* (or $`CH`$-removable) if every homeomorphism of the Riemann sphere that is conformal on $`\widehat{\mathbb C}\setminus E`$ is a Möbius transformation.

**Question.** If $`E`$ and $`F`$ are conformally removable compact sets in the plane, must $`E\cup F`$ also be conformally removable? The unresolved case permits $`E\cap F\ne\varnothing`$. The answer is known to be yes when $`E`$ and $`F`$ are disjoint.

Equivalently, can there be a non-Möbius sphere homeomorphism that is conformal outside the union of two individually removable compact sets?

## Application

Removability lets one extend conformal rigidity across exceptional sets. A union theorem would permit such arguments to be assembled across overlapping pieces of a boundary, notably in conformal welding and holomorphic dynamics. The question is about global homeomorphisms, not just extension of bounded holomorphic functions.

## Status review


**Known cases:** The exact gap concerns overlapping arbitrary conformally removable compacta. Special SLE curves, disjoint unions and rigidity of circle domains with already removable boundaries do not prove general union closure.
