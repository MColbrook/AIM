# 1004. Hörmander densities for equations driven by measurable Markovian rough paths

**Area:** Rough paths and singular stochastic equations
**Status:** 🟡 PARTIAL
**Last checked:** 2026-10-30

## Problem statement

Let $`d,e\ge1`$, $`N\ge2`$, and let $`G^N(\mathbb R^d)`$ be the free step-$`N`$ nilpotent Lie group with its horizontal left-invariant generators $`U_1,\ldots,U_d`$ and Haar measure $`\lambda`$. Let $`a:G^N(\mathbb R^d)\to\mathrm{Sym}_d`$ be measurable, with

```math
\Lambda^{-1}|\xi|^2\le \xi^Ta(x)\xi\le\Lambda|\xi|^2
\quad(x\in G^N(\mathbb R^d),\ \xi\in\mathbb R^d)
```


for some finite $`\Lambda\ge1`$. Let $`\mathbf X=\mathbf X^{a,x_0}`$ be the canonical Markovian rough-path diffusion of Chevyrev–Ogrodnik §1.1, associated with the closure in $`L^2(\lambda)`$ of

```math
\mathcal E(f,g)=\int\sum_{i,j=1}^d a_{ij}(x)(U_if)(x)(U_jg)(x)\,d\lambda(x),
\qquad f,g\in C_c^\infty(G^N(\mathbb R^d)).
```


Interpret this diffusion as a geometric rough path, as in the cited source. Let $`V_1,\ldots,V_d\in C_b^\infty(\mathbb R^e;\mathbb R^e)`$, and suppose their iterated Lie brackets span $`\mathbb R^e`$ at every point. For arbitrary deterministic initial states $`x_0\in G^N(\mathbb R^d)`$ and $`y_0\in\mathbb R^e`$, solve

```math
dY_t=\sum_{i=1}^d V_i(Y_t)\,d\mathbf X_t^i,\qquad Y_0=y_0.
```


Must the law of $`Y_t`$ be absolutely continuous with respect to Lebesgue measure on $`\mathbb R^e`$ for every $`t>0`$?

This is the full-dimensional, bounded-smooth-vector-field form of the question in Chevyrev–Ogrodnik, Remark 3.11. The source also asks the corresponding question on the orbit generated by the vector fields. No smoothness of $`a`$, constant rank of brackets of length greater than $`N`$, or smoothness of the resulting density is required here.

## Application

The equation describes a nonlinear system driven by diffusion through a medium whose local covariance can be merely measurable. A density permits probabilities for the system's state to be represented by integration, even when ordinary stochastic differential-equation formulas requiring smooth noise coefficients are unavailable. The question tests whether the usual bracket condition alone supplies that property for this class of rough inputs.
