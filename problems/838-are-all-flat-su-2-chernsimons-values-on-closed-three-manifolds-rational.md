# 838. Are all flat SU(2) Chern–Simons values on closed three-manifolds rational?

**Area:** Gauge theory and three-manifold topology
**Status:** 🟡 PARTIAL
**Last checked:** 2026-10-30

## Problem statement

Let $`A`$ be a flat connection on the trivial $`SU(2)`$ bundle over a closed oriented three-manifold $`M`$. Is its Chern–Simons invariant $`CS(M,A)`$ always rational (with the usual normalization modulo integers)? Equivalently, can an exact irrational critical value occur?

## Application

Known rationality results cover Seifert-fibered spaces and graph manifolds. An irrational value would distinguish some more general three-manifold topology from those classes. A specific hyperbolic surgery is a numerical candidate, but its irrationality remains conjectural.

## Status review


**Known cases:** Graph-manifold and Seifert rationality do not cover every three-manifold. A high-precision numerical candidate cannot establish irrationality of an exact SU2 critical value. Complex SL2 Chern-Simons values are not counterexamples for the compact SU2 target.

Their roughly 50-decimal-place approximation for an invariant $`r_s`$ of $`1/2`$ surgery on the mirror of knot $`5_2`$ motivates an irrationality conjecture, but a decimal approximation cannot settle exact rationality.
