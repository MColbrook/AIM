# 792. Does a one-cusped finite-volume real hyperbolic manifold exist above dimension four?

**Area:** Hyperbolic geometry and geometric topology
**Status:** 🟡 PARTIAL
**Last checked:** 2026-10-30

## Problem statement

Is there a complete finite-volume **real** hyperbolic $`n`$-manifold with exactly **one cusp** for any $`n\geq5`$? The broader dimension-by-dimension question asks for which such $`n`$ one exists.

## Application

One-cusped manifolds exist in dimensions two, three and four. A higher-dimensional example would show that a single cusp is compatible with hyperbolic geometry beyond four dimensions. It would also sharpen the contrast with obstructions for some arithmetic orbifolds in high dimensions.
