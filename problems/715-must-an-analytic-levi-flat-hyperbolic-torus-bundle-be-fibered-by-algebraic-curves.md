# 715. Must an analytic Levi-flat hyperbolic torus bundle be fibered by algebraic curves?

**Area:** Complex geometry and foliation theory
**Status:** 🔵 OPEN
**Last checked:** 2026-10-30

## Problem statement

Let $`X`$ be a complex algebraic surface and $`M\subset X`$ an embedded real-analytic Levi-flat hypersurface. Suppose $`M`$ is diffeomorphic to a hyperbolic torus bundle over the circle: a $`T^2`$-bundle whose monodromy has no eigenvalue of modulus one. Must the Cauchy–Riemann foliation of $`M`$ be a fibration by algebraic curves?

The source calls a Levi-flat hypersurface with such a fibration *periodic* [1, Introduction]. The question concerns the foliation induced by the **embedding in an algebraic surface**, not an arbitrary analytic foliation on the same three-manifold.

## Application

An affirmative answer would turn a topological condition on a real hypersurface into a rigid algebraic description of every complex leaf. It would also identify which foliation dynamics can occur on this class of Levi-flat hypersurfaces in algebraic surfaces.

## Known results and scope

Canales González proves that the Cauchy–Riemann foliation has at least one compact leaf, an elliptic curve [1, Theorem 6.10]. This is weaker than every leaf being a fiber. The same paper exhibits abstract analytic foliations on hyperbolic torus bundles with both compact and noncompact leaves [1, Proposition 6.11]; it does **not** embed these as the Cauchy–Riemann foliation of a Levi-flat hypersurface in an algebraic surface. This distinction is why that proposition does not refute the conjecture.

## References

## Status review


### Online recheck, 2026-10-30

The conjecture concerns the CR foliation of an analytic Levi-flat embedded in an algebraic surface. Abstract foliations on the same torus bundle and existence of one compact leaf do not establish the required algebraic fibration.
